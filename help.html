Help Center

Getting Started

  • How do I create an account?
    Click “Sign up” on the login page, fill the form and submit.
  • Which user type should I pick?
    Filmmaker = student / creator; Mentor = industry professional; Sponsor = donor / company.
  • I forgot my password.
    Use the “Forgot password” link on the sign-in page.

Courses & Learning

  • Are courses free?
    Yes – all courses on the demo platform are free.
  • Can I download videos?
    Not yet; streaming only.
  • How do I get a certificate?
    Complete 100 % of the lessons and the final quiz.

Mentorship

  • How long does a mentorship last?
    You choose: 3 months, 6 months, 1 year or ongoing.
  • Can I change mentor?
    Yes – email support@cinda.rw and we’ll re-match you.
  • Are sessions recorded?
    Only if both sides agree; by default they are not.
